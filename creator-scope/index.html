Questions, answered

Know what you're signing up for.

Is CreatorScope operated by YouTube or Twitch?

No. CreatorScope is an independent assessment concept. YouTube and Twitch are trademarks of their respective owners; CreatorScope is not affiliated with or endorsed by either platform.

Does an assessment guarantee growth or monetization?

No. An assessment can identify potential opportunities and recommend actions, but audience growth, revenue, and platform decisions are never guaranteed.

Are the CreatorPulse numbers live?

Not yet. The current page is a preview interface. Live counts and available capacity will only be displayed after a real database and review workflow are connected.

What happens after I submit an application?

This prototype currently demonstrates the application experience in your browser only. It does not send or store applications. A secure backend and notification workflow must be configured before accepting real submissions.
