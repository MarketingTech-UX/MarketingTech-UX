Questions, answered

Know what you're signing up for.

Is CreatorScope operated by YouTube or Twitch?

No. CreatorScope is an independent concept and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by YouTube, Twitch, or their parent companies.

Does an assessment guarantee growth or monetization?

No. An assessment can identify opportunities and practical next steps, but results depend on many factors. We do not guarantee audience growth, income, partnership status, or monetization approval.

Are the CreatorPulse numbers live?

Not in this prototype. Live totals, review statuses, and available slots require a connected database and verified application records. This page intentionally does not invent those numbers.

What happens after I submit an application?

This prototype demonstrates the intake interface only. Submissions are not stored or emailed yet. Production application handling will be enabled after a secure backend and privacy notice are implemented.
